About North Group Holding

One Group. Multiple Businesses. One Vision.

North Group Holding is a parent group based in Mikindani, Mombasa County, Kenya. The group brings together businesses serving customers across beauty, children's fashion, fragrances and cereals.

Through its family of brands, North Group Holding aims to develop its businesses, build lasting customer relationships and pursue new opportunities for growth.

Meet Our Chairman

Collince Mbanda

A young entrepreneur with an ambition to build, develop and grow a diverse family of businesses. His leadership represents the group's commitment to entrepreneurship, opportunity and long-term growth.
